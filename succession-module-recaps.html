October 12 Summary & Memory Aid — Disinheritance, Intestacy & Representation (Arts. 915–923, 960–1014)

Module 1 Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Disinheritance Requisites & Grounds (Arts. 915–923): Seven requisites (W-E-L-S-T-U-T), burden of proof on other heirs (Art. 917), 8 child grounds (Art. 919), 8 parent grounds (Art. 920), 6 spouse grounds (Art. 921 + FC Art. 55/63[4]), reconciliation (Art. 922), and representation (Art. 923).
  • Ineffectual Disinheritance vs. Preterition (Art. 918 vs. Art. 854): Three ₱120M computation scenarios comparing total annulment of institution, restoration of legitime alone, and valid disinheritance with/without representation.
  • Intestacy, Representation & Iron Curtain (Arts. 960–1014, 777): Proximity (Art. 962), regular/irregular orders, concurrence fractions, per stirpes vs. per capita (Art. 969), Arts. 976 vs. 977, Aquino v. Aquino (Art. 992), R.A. 11642 Secs. 41 & 43, and Dela Merced (Art. 777).
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic Source Rule & Articles
W–E–L–S–T–U–T Lecture 7 Disinheritance Checkpoints (Arts. 915–918): Valid Will, Express, Legal cause, Stated, True & certain, Unconditional, Total deprivation of legitime.
977 vs. 976 Lecture 977 — Repudiator cannot BE represented; 976 — You CAN represent someone whose own estate you previously rejected.
Parent loses, descendants may represent Lecture Art. 923: Disinherited heir gets ₱0 and loses parental usufruct/administration, while descendants inherit the legitime.
Class First, Degree Second Study Aid Art. 962 & Arts. 978–1014: Privileged collateral class (nephew, 3rd deg.) defeats non-privileged collateral (uncle, 3rd deg.).
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Art. 918 vs. Art. 854: Defective disinheritance restores only the disinherited heir's legitime (D=₱15M; A,B,C=₱35M each); omitting another direct-line compulsory heir triggers preterition, annulling the institution into total intestacy (₱30M each).
  • Current Law on Representation (Aquino & R.A. 11642): Nonmarital grandchildren may represent a predeceased parent in direct-line succession (Aquino); adopted children carry extended legitimate filiation to the adopter's parents, siblings, and descendants (Sec. 41).
  • Dela Merced Death Sequence (Art. 777): Evarista died first (1987) → Francisco inherited immediately → Francisco died later (1988) → Joselito inherits directly from Francisco's vested estate, bypassing Art. 992.
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Attempt on Life (919[1]) vs. Maltreatment (919[6]): Paragraph (1) requires criminal conviction; without prosecution, switch to paragraph (6) maltreatment by deed. Likewise, an acquittal is NOT automatically a "groundless" accusation under (2).
  • Art. 920(8) vs. Art. 920(2) Reconciliation: Reconciliation between parents eliminates paragraph (8), not a separate convicted attempt under paragraph (2); and Art. 922 requires genuine bilateral reconciliation, not mere unilateral deathbed words or a future promise.
  • Art. 969 Division: When ALL children repudiate, grandchildren inherit in their own right per capita (not per stirpes).

Legacies & Devises Summary & Memory Aid (Arts. 924–959)

Module 2 Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Distinctions, Burdens & Warranties (Arts. 924–928, 854): Heir vs. legatee/devisee (Trinidad preterition survival), sub-legacy caps (Art. 925), solidary negligent loss (Art. 927), and eviction warranty for generic things (Art. 928).
  • Ownership, Mortgages & Credits (Arts. 929–939): Part-owned, stranger-owned (Trinidad 2023), legatee-owned/acquired (Arts. 929–933), estate payment of secured debts (Art. 934), credit/remission legacies, and Art. 936 suit lapse.
  • Specific vs. Generic, Reduction, Delivery & Revocation (Arts. 940–959, 911): Art. 941 movable vs. realty rule, Art. 948 death vesting, Art. 911 vs. Art. 950, delivery (Art. 953), election (Arts. 954–955), merging (Art. 956; Rigor), T-A-L revocation (Art. 957; Fernandez), and Art. 959 relatives.
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic Source Rule & Articles
Death = Ownership + Fruits + Risk Lecture Art. 948: Specific determinate thing belonging to T vests at death with post-death fruits and risk of fortuitous loss.
R–P–S–E–S–A Lecture Art. 950 payment order: Remuneratory, Preferential, Support, Education, Specific, All others pro rata.
Art. 911 = PROTECT; Art. 950 = PRIORITIZE Lecture Use Art. 911 when legitimes are impaired; use Art. 950 only when legitimes are intact and legacies compete over free-portion insufficiency.
T–A–L Lecture Art. 957 revocation/ineffectiveness: Transformation, Alienation, Loss.
Demand Letter ≠ Judicial Action Study Aid Art. 936: Only filing a judicial suit against the debtor revokes a legacy of credit/remission.
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Generic Movable vs. Indeterminate Realty (Art. 941): Generic personal legacy (e.g., 10 cows) is valid even if absent from the estate; indeterminate realty requires immovable property of that kind in the estate.
  • Ownership Matrix (Arts. 929–933): Stranger's thing by mistake is void (Art. 930; Trinidad); ordered purchase obliges estate to buy/pay just value (Art. 931); legatee's later onerous acquisition earns reimbursement (Art. 933).
  • Delivery & Merging (Arts. 953, 956): Legatee cannot self-help take possession despite death ownership; failed particular gifts merge into the estate unless substitution or accretion applies.
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Applying Art. 950 When Legitimes Are Impaired: In the ₱40,000-estate example with ₱20,000 legitime and ₱40,000 legacies, Art. 911 pro rata reduction governs, NOT R-P-S-E-S-A!
  • Reacquisition After Sale (Art. 957[2]): Buying back a devised property through an ordinary purchase does NOT revive the devise (only exercising a right of repurchase or vitiated consent under Fernandez saves it).
  • General Gift to "Relatives" (Art. 959): Strictly favors blood relatives nearest in degree (living brother excludes his children/nephews, and spouse is not a blood relative here).
  • Education vs. Support (Art. 944): Education does not automatically end at age 18 if diligently pursued to finish the course.

October 19 Summary & Memory Aid — Intestacy, Mixed Succession & Accretion (Arts. 960–1023)

Module 3 Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Intestate Triggers & Share Combinations (Arts. 960, 996–1001, 1006–1009): Four statutory intestacy causes, Santillon/Macalinao 1/2 spouse rule (Art. 996), full vs. half-blood 2:1 sibling/nephew rule (Arts. 1006, 1008), and Concurrence Theory (Art. 983 + FC Art. 176).
  • Mixed Succession & Accretion (Arts. 1015–1023): Sarah ₱10M legacy computation protecting parents' and illegitimate children's legitimes against Wife W's excess intestate share; U-P-RIP accretion vs. physical earmarking.
  • ₱120M Predecease + Repudiation & Case Corrections (Arts. 970–977, 981, 1019, 1021, R.A. 11642): Professor Views A/B vs. strict statutory analysis, Aquino, Dela Merced (1987 → 1988), and De los Santos (3rd vs. 4th degree).
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic Source Rule & Articles
U–P–RIP Lecture Art. 1016 Accretion: Unity of object, Plurality pro indiviso, Repudiation, Incapacity, Predecease.
Fractions Yes, Earmarking No Lecture Art. 1017: Abstract aliquot shares allow accretion; physical earmarking (north vs. south half) prevents it.
Full Estate First, Legacy Second Study Aid Mixed Succession: Always compute compulsory legitimes on the full net hereditary estate (₱120M), never after subtracting the legacy (₱110M).
Protect Legitime → Split Surplus 2:2:1:1 Study Aid Concurrence Theory (Art. 983 + FC Art. 176; Macalinao): Reserve LCs' 1/2 and ICs' half-shares first, then split surplus proportionally.
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Art. 996 vs. 888/892 (Santillon & Macalinao 2024): In pure intestacy with 1 LC + 1 Spouse, both receive 1/2 (not 1/2 and 1/4).
  • Mixed Succession Method (Sarah's ₱10M Legacy): Parents (₱60M) and ICs (₱30M) have intestate shares equal to their legitimes; only Wife W (₱30M intestate vs. ₱15M legitime) has a ₱15M excess to absorb Sarah's ₱10M legacy, leaving W with ₱20M.
  • Classroom Views vs. Strict Statutory View on ₱120M Problem: Lecture Views A & B pass C's entire ₱30M (legitime + free) to E & F (42.5M/17.5M or 45M/15M), whereas the strict statutory approach limits E & F to C's legitime (A=₱50M, B=₱50M, E=₱10M, F=₱10M) and in pure intestacy gives E & F ₱20M each under Art. 1019.
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Deducting a Legacy Before Computing Legitimes: Never subtract Sarah's ₱10M from ₱120M before calculating compulsory legitimes!
  • Applying Double Share to Distant Collaterals: The 2:1 full- vs. half-blood ratio (Arts. 1006, 1008) stops at siblings and nephews/nieces—Art. 1009 eliminates it for other collaterals.
  • Old Case/Transcript Errors: Remember Dela Merced dates (Evarista died 1987, Francisco 1988—NOT Francisco predeceasing) and De los Santos degrees (nephew = 3rd degree, grandniece = 4th degree).

Section XII–XIII Summary & Memory Aid — Right of Accretion & Vacancy Matrix (Arts. 1015–1023)

Section XII–XIII Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Four Vacancy Mechanisms & Testamentary Accretion (Arts. 1015–1017, 1019, 1020): Substitution, representation, accretion, and intestacy; pro indiviso vs. earmarked gifts (Art. 1017), proportional division (Art. 1019), and accompanying obligations (Art. 1020).
  • Intestate Accretion & Protected Legitime (Arts. 1018, 1021, 968–970, 977): Bar on representing repudiators (Art. 977), next-degree inheritance in own right (Art. 969), and prohibition against testamentary accretion over repudiated legitime (Art. 1021).
  • Master Vacancy Matrix & ₱120M Problem (Arts. 888, 971, 974, 981, 1018–1021): Testate distribution (A=₱50M, B=₱50M, E=₱10M, F=₱10M, G=₱0) vs. corrected pure intestate distribution (A=₱40M, B=₱40M, E=₱20M, F=₱20M, G=₱0).
  • Advanced Exam Traps (Arts. 777, 859, 960, 1022, 1053): Substitute overrides accretion; postdecease transmits choice rather than accruing; total failure passes to legal heirs.
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic / Cue Source Rule & Statutory Anchor
R-I-P Lecture Qualifying causes for testamentary accretion (Art. 1016): Repudiation, Incapacity, Predecease.
4-Phrase Memory System Lecture (1) "The legitime is protected"; (2) "The will's substitute comes first"; (3) "A repudiating heir cannot be represented"; (4) "Count family branches before counting grandchildren."
Burdens Follow Benefit Lecture Art. 1020: Accrued share does not necessarily arrive free from attached testamentary obligations.
S → R → A → I (Read the Will First) Study Aid Order of triage in free portion: Substitution (Art. 859) → Accretion (Art. 1016) → Intestacy (Arts. 960, 1022). In intestacy, Representation blocks branch vacancy before accretion.
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Two Requisites of Art. 1016: (1) Two or more persons called to the same inheritance or portion pro indiviso (aliquot fractions allowed under Art. 1017; earmarked objects excluded); (2) R-I-P vacancy without an effective substitute.
  • Proportional Accretion (Art. 1019): Co-heirs absorb the vacant share in the exact ratio of their original institutions (e.g., 3:2 gives ₱6M/₱4M, not 50/50).
  • Why Testate vs. Intestate ₱120M Differ: Under a will, E and F represent C only in the ₱60M collective legitime (₱20M branch → ₱10M each under Art. 1021 own-right redistribution), while A and B absorb both C's and D's ₱15M free portions (₱50M each). In pure intestacy, E and F represent C for the whole branch (Arts. 971, 974) and share D's ₱30M equally per branch (₱40M / ₱40M / ₱20M / ₱20M).
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Excluding Represented Branches in Intestate Accretion: Do not allocate D's repudiated ₱30M only to A and B (₱45M/₱45M/₱15M/₱15M); E and F are heirs by representation and share D's portion through C's branch (₱40M/₱40M/₱20M/₱20M).
  • Treating Lack of Accretion as Automatic Immediate Intestacy: Always check substitutes, residuary provisions, and Arts. 956, 960, and 1022 first.
  • Confusing Postdecease with Predecease: If an heir survives X on Jan 1 and dies Jan 10 undecided, apply Arts. 777 and 1053 (transmission), never accretion!
  • Over-reading Art. 1016 vs. Art. 968: Failed suspensive conditions (Art. 960[3]) and unknown heirs (Art. 845) are distinct from R-I-P; and Art. 968 expressly mentions accrual in intestacy for unwillingness or incapacity subject to representation.

Section XIV Summary & Memory Aid — Capacity, Undue Influence & Unworthiness (Arts. 1024–1040)

Section XIV Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Three Qualification Gates & Unborn/Simultaneous Rules (Arts. 40, 41, 43, 1024–1025): Complete delivery rule for conceived fetuses (<7 mos = 24-hour survival; ≥7 mos = born alive) and simultaneous death presumption (Art. 43).
  • Relative Incapacity & Prohibited Gifts (Arts. 1027, 1028, 739, 823, 1031): Six undue-influence categories, guardian/witness exceptions, adultery/concubinage/criminal/public-officer bars, and prohibition of disguised intermediaries.
  • Unworthiness, Pardon & Collateral Rules (Arts. 1032–1040, 16, 921, 922, 1001): Eight grounds of unworthiness, Paramour Paradox, Art. 1033 pardon vs. Art. 922 reconciliation, Art. 1035 protection of descendants, O-A-I-C nationality rule, and 5-year recovery period (Art. 1040).
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic Source Rule & Articles
P–R–G–W–M (+ Others) Lecture Art. 1027: Priest/Minister, 4th-deg Relatives/Church, Guardian, Witness + kin, Medical personnel, plus others barred by law.
Bad parent → Life → Accusation → Silence → Affair → Forced will → Blocked will → Forged will Lecture The 8 sequential grounds of unworthiness under Art. 1032(1)–(8).
1033 = Knowledge or Writing; 922 = Reconciliation Lecture Pardon of unworthiness (Art. 1033) vs. restoration of disinheritance (Art. 922).
O–A–I–C Lecture National law of decedent (Arts. 16, 1039): Order, Amount, Intrinsic validity, Capacity to succeed.
Gift vs. Status Study Aid Art. 1027 disqualifies only the free-portion gift (legitime intact); Art. 1032 forfeits succession itself (testate, intestate, and legitime).
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Complete Delivery vs. Cord Cut (Art. 41): Statutory clock starts at complete delivery from the mother's womb; <7 months requires 24-hour survival, ≥7 months needs only live birth.
  • Guardian & Witness Carve-Outs: Family guardians (ascendant, descendant, sibling, spouse) are expressly exempt under Art. 1027(3); an unrelated guardian's gift is tested at execution of the will. A gift to an attesting witness is void unless 3 other competent witnesses exist (Art. 823).
  • Criminal Conviction Timing (Art. 739[1] vs. Art. 1032[2],[3],[5]): Adultery/concubinage under Art. 739(1) needs only preponderance of evidence; Art. 1032(2) and (5) require final judgment of conviction (Art. 1034).
  • Action Period (Art. 1040): 5 years from the disqualified person's taking possession.
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Priest Praying the Rosary (Art. 1027[1]): Distinguish sacramental confession (priest) from spiritual aid (minister of the gospel) under the literal statutory text.
  • Physician-Son Controversy (Art. 1027[5]): Unlike Art. 1027(3) guardians, paragraph (5) has no express family-member exception; discuss both the strict statutory view and the professor's family-duty view.
  • Paramour Paradox (Art. 1032[5]): Disqualifies the convicted paramour, not the unfaithful spouse under that literal paragraph—check Art. 1001 (1/2 split with siblings), legal separation, and Art. 921(4) disinheritance.
  • Verbal Forgiveness & Simultaneous Death: Verbal forgiveness never cures Art. 1032 unworthiness (Art. 1033 requires will-knowledge or writing); and Art. 43 simultaneous death still allows grandchildren to inherit directly by representation.

Section XV Summary & Memory Aid — Acceptance & Repudiation (Arts. 1041–1057)

Section XV Recap
1. Topics Discussed & Statutory Scope
  • Core Principles & Capacities (Arts. 1041–1048): Voluntariness, retroactivity to death, certainty of death and right, capacity rules for minors/wards (Guy v. CA), corporations, public establishments, married women, and special rules under Art. 1048.
  • Forms, Waivers & Creditor Protection (Arts. 1049–1052): Express/tacit acceptance vs. formal repudiation, preservation acts, Art. 1050 deemed acceptance traps, and Art. 1052 creditor intervention.
  • Transmission, Dual Capacity, Presumptive Death & Deadlines (Arts. 1053–1057, 390–392, 954–955, 1335): Transmission vs. representation, asymmetric repudiation, 7/10/5/4 presumptive death periods, irrevocability exceptions, 30-day distribution order rule, and onerous legacies.
2. Mnemonics Cheat Sheet
Mnemonic Source Rule & Articles
Death + Right Lecture Art. 1043: Must be certain of both the decedent's death and your right to inherit before accepting or repudiating.
PRICE or PREFERENCE = ACCEPTANCE Lecture Art. 1050: Receiving consideration or selecting specific co-heirs converts a "waiver" into deemed acceptance.
Representation = BEFORE; Transmission = AFTER, undecided Lecture Art. 970 vs. Art. 1053: Check whether the original heir died before or after the decedent.
7 – 10 – 5 – 4 Lecture Presumptive death (Arts. 390–391): 7 non-succession; 10 ordinary succession; 5 after age 75; 4 danger of death.
30 Days From Court Order (NOT Death) Study Aid Art. 1057: Silence for 30 days after the court's order for distribution triggers deemed acceptance.
3. Important Concepts, Elements & Operative Rules
  • Formalities (Art. 1049 vs. Art. 1051): Acceptance may be express (public or private document) or tacit (acts of ownership); repudiation MUST be express and formal (public/authentic instrument or court petition).
  • Asymmetric Dual Capacity (Art. 1055): Repudiating as testamentary heir repudiates both capacities; repudiating as intestate heir without knowledge of the will still allows accepting under the will.
  • Minor's Protection (Art. 1044 & Guy v. CA): Parents/guardians may accept for minors, but cannot repudiate without judicial authorization; public establishments need government approval for both (Art. 1046).
  • Irrevocability Exceptions (Art. 1056): Irrevocable except for vitiated consent (excluding lawful-claim threats under Art. 1335) or appearance of an unknown will.
4. Top Quiz Traps to Avoid
  • Preservation vs. Tacit Acceptance (Art. 1049): Paying utility bills or fixing a leaking roof to preserve estate property is NOT acceptance unless the heir's title/capacity is assumed.
  • Creditor Excess Under Art. 1052: After prejudiced creditors take what covers their debts, any remaining balance goes to the next legal successors—NEVER back to the renouncing heir (and Art. 1052 covers the heir's creditors, not estate creditors).
  • 7-Year Trap (Art. 390) & Past Fruits (Art. 392): 7 years of ordinary absence does NOT open succession (wait 10 years, or 5 if past age 75); returning absentees recover property/price, never past fruits or rents.
  • Compulsory Heir vs. Separate Legacy (Arts. 954–955): You cannot take a gratuitous legacy and reject an onerous legacy, but a compulsory heir MAY waive the inheritance and accept a separate legacy (or vice versa).
